The world as it is

The Emberfall

✦
I

The Weave broke.

The lattice that held all magic shattered and rained down as embers — glowing shards of broken power. Kingdoms fell, the beasts warped, and the wilds swallowed the roads.

II

The Hearths held.

Survivors raised lantern-ringed frontier towns and learned the trick that changed everything: an ember bound into a weapon can be channelled. You are what you wield.

III

The wound is still open.

At the Sundered Heart the Weave still bleeds — and the man who tried to mend it bound a hundred embers and forgot how to die. Someone has to walk the road.

1 · Diagnosis

Player complaint: "super limited in choices, everything feels extremely samey."

Root cause locked through grilling:

  • Shape-samey (primary) — every existing weapon mechanic is "modify a hit." Combo += 1 on landed hit; Momentum += 1 on landed hit; element rider modifies on-hit outcome; ambush ×2 the next hit. None of the four meshes produce a persistent map presence, none introduce a temporal/positional pattern, none have a defensive/reactive shape. Different gauges, same shape family.
  • Path-samey (symptom of shape-samey) — Vanguard/Berserker, Marksman/Skirmisher etc. feel mechanically alike because the underlying ability shapes within each path are all "cooldown → hit + tag" variations.
  • Sword specifically — too many self-buffs. 3 self-buffs (Iron Riposte, Bulwark, Vow) reading "press button, gain timed buff." Other weapons have 1-2.
  • Bow specifically — only one momentum-spender. Most of the kit is build-build-build-Snipe.
  • Dagger specifically — stealth is too binary. ×2 ambush concentrates all value in one entry hit → either ignore stealth or chain-cycle for the one big hit. Neither plays well.
  • Staff specifically — already designed; just lacked the (c) signature transformations on element-named spells.

V1 fix surface: introduce ground-zone shape category (cross-weapon infrastructure) + spread stealth value across a window (medium fix) + add per-weapon shape gaps via 4 new abilities each + 1 gauge-engaging passive each.

3 · Staff — stance transformations + 5 new actives locked

Staff

Gauge: Element Stance (Fire/Ice/Lightning) 8 → 13 actives · 6 → 7 passives · stat axis: Magic Attack

Approach: existing element rider stays (Fire burn / Ice slow / Lightning chain on every staff hit). Layer (c) signature transformations on the elemental-named spells when cast in their matched stance. Mismatched stance casts = base spell only (β bonus-not-requirement rule). R cycle direction = forward only (Fire → Ice → Lightning → Fire). Then add 5 new actives to equalize at 13.

Transformations (augments to existing abilities)

SpellStanceStance-matched transformationMismatch behavior
Glacial SpikeIceTarget frozen 1.0–1.5s (hard CC, cannot act).Damage only, no freeze. Existing Ice rider's slow still applies via baseline.
Pyre BurstFire3-4s ground-zone fire pool at impact, ticking ~10% MATK/s on enemies inside. (Existing +1 Burn stack stays.)Burst AoE damage only. No pool.
Arcane LanceLightningEach pulse of the channel fires its OWN chain rider. 3 pulses × 3 hops = up to 9 secondary chain hits per cast.Single linear channel; existing Lightning rider still fires from the main hit.
ImmolateFireApplies 2 Burn stacks/cast instead of 1, AND spreads existing Burn to +1 nearby enemy.Normal 1-stack apply.

Stance switching

  • Toggle cost: ~10-15 MP per cycle, no CD. Small but real (≈half a Glacial Spike's mana). Stays in a stance for the right purpose; can flex mid-fight.
  • Mismatch rule: (β) — bonus-not-requirement. Stance-matched spells get their transformation; mismatched casts do base damage. No ability is locked out.
  • Stance-agnostic spells: Aether Ward, Phase Step, Communion. None deal damage on hit, so the baseline rider has nothing to attach to and there's no transformation. They cast identically in any stance — by design.
  • L1 starting stance: Fire (already default in code; volatile, resets to Fire on spawn).

9 · Upgrade pattern + sample tree locked

Each new ability (and each existing one) follows the PR 6 upgrade shape:

  • T1 (1pt, fixed-always-owned) — small flat improvement on one axis.
  • T2 (1pt, fixed-always-owned) — medium flat improvement on a different axis.
  • T3 (2pt, pick ONE of 3-5 mutually-exclusive variants) — each variant changes how the ability plays, not just "+more damage." Variants must be genuinely close in power AND mechanically distinct (per balance report finding 8: "+1 hit beats +90% damage").

Total surface: ~60-65 new upgrade variants across the 12 new actives + 4 staff transformations.

10 · Design constraints learned this session

Enemy damage is collision-based, not telegraphed

Only Goblin has a telegraphed attack; the rest of the enemy roster deals damage by walking into the player. Reactive parry / dodge-frame / perfect-block shapes do not fit this game's enemy design. The whole "reactive timing window" shape category is off the table for ability proposals unless and until the enemy roster gains telegraphed attacks.

Logic-script for behavior variation, not schema fields

Per-ability behavior variation rides on logic_script hooks (execute / on_hit / on_proc / on_tick). Don't add bools to shared schemas (AbilityData, ActiveBehaviorData) for behaviors that only apply to a subset of resources. Ground-zone follows this rule.

Burn pools are per-ability, not unified

Existing convention: each Fire-stance Staff ability has its own meta-keyed burn pool (staff_element_burn, pyre_burst_burn, Immolate's own). Independence-by-meta is the project's stacking pattern — don't unify pools across abilities.

Each weapon owns a stat axis

Sword = Defense, Bow = Accuracy, Dagger = Evasion, Staff = Magic Attack. Don't duplicate the same passive shape across weapons even with different names.

Passives are class-neutral (stack from both weapons)

Passives from both equipped weapon slots stack — their effects must work without assuming which weapon is wielded. Class-locked behaviors belong on actives or per-ability upgrades, NOT on passives.

Class gauges live on the screen edge, not in the hotbar

Sword combo, bow Momentum, staff stance, dagger stealth all get their own anchored class-widget. Don't try to fit gauge UI into the hotbar's horizontal budget.

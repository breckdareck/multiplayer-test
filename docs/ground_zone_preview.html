Player (~40×80 px) Floor line (zone center) 1 SVG unit = 1 in-game pixel

Shape choice rationale — in this 2D platformer, a ground-zone ability should hug the floor and overlap with enemy hitboxes at their height, not tower above them.

Rectangle (wide x, short y, ≈30–65 px tall) reads correctly for tremors, scatter patches, fire pools, rain strikes — anything bound to the ground plane.

Circle reads correctly only when the effect is meant to be radial / volumetric — Banner of the Vanguard's aura halo, Smoke Bomb's billowing cloud.

Verify in-game with /zone <name> in the debug console (backtick to open).

In-game visual indicators — the fill alone reads ambiguously, so every zone gets three layered effects (implemented in GroundZone._draw / _physics_process, applied to every zone for free):

Spawn flash — first ~0.3s after spawn the fill + outline brighten loudly so the zone's appearance is unmistakable at any distance.

Tick pulse — on each damage tick the zone flashes brighter for ~0.25s (decays via _TICK_PULSE_DECAY). Player gets both the damage rhythm AND a re-emphasis of the area extent on the beat.

Persistent boundary markers — drawn at ~95% alpha so the boundary stays unmistakable even when the fill fades toward end-of-life:

RECT: 14-px L-shapes at all 4 corners

CIRCLE: 8 radial ticks at compass points

Tick-pulse cadence is synchronized on every peer (server + remote-mirror clones) because the tick countdown lives above the is_server gate in _physics_process — both peers init _next_tick = tick_interval in _ready and decrement at the same rate.

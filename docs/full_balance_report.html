Executive summary

This report re-implements the live combat pipeline (combat.gd._execute_hit / _calculate_max_range + stats.gd) and drives it with the authoritative per-level ability numbers dumped straight from the game formulas (docs/ability_data_dump.json). For every damage ability it walks ranks 1→max at the character level a focused player unlocks them, against a spread of monster levels, from level 1 to 100.

☑ RESOLVED (2026-06-08): the 7 raw-stat riders are fixed. Watch tuning on Earthsplitter at max combo (combo amp × the now-larger base) and Spellweave's amplified release — both are strong but no longer broken.

Methodology & assumptions (click)
  • Damage formula: max_range = round(1.2 × (primary×4 + secondary) × attack_power / 100); per hit rolls randf_range(0.2×max, max) (avg 0.6×), then × level-modifier clamp(1+0.05×(charLv-mobLv), 0.5, 1.5), × defense 1 - DEF/(DEF+500), × expected crit. DoT/zone ticks call take_damage directly so they skip defense, crit, level-mod and always land.
  • Player build: focused single weapon, all 5 attribute points/level into the primary damage stat (soft-capped above 300 allocation), plus per-mastery stat bonuses. Mastery tracks the character level and caps at 100 around char 70 (focused grinder).
  • Gear: WEAPON/MAGIC ATTACK is base 0 (gear only); modelled ~9 at L1 → ~197 at L100 (real starter → endgame weapons). Folded in 2026-06-08: the level-appropriate generated SET (4 armour pieces + weapon, generate_item_content.gd) now contributes its full attribute spread (STR/DEX/INT/LUCK/CON), crit chance, and crit damage to the player stats — so these numbers reflect a geared player, not attributes+mastery alone (this adds ~+16% max-range / ~+20% damage at L100, and pushes gear-stacked crit rates high, e.g. Dagger ~100% from LUCK→crit + Nightshade crit gear).
  • Rank pacing: ability rank r is shown at character level round(70×r/max_level) — where a focused player realistically has that rank (rank max ≈ char 70).
  • Enemies: NONE-archetype curve baseline — HP 0.7×L^2.3, DEF/MDEF 1.8×L^1.2. Real enemies apply archetype multipliers (tanky/glassy) on top.
  • % HP = single cast / single DoT application vs ONE at-level enemy. Green ≥25%, amber 8–25%, red <8%. DoT stacks & AoE-on-many-targets are noted, not folded into the single-target % HP.

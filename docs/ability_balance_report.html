Ability Damage & Balance Report

Weapon-identity overhaul · branch pr-7-attributes-class-removal · model mirrors the verified combat.gd / stats.gd formula (2026-05-29)

The damage formula (verified)

max_range = 1.2 × (primary×4 + secondary) × ATTACK_POWER / 100
roll      = rand(max_range × 0.2 , max_range)        # 20%–100% of max
ability   = roll × (damage_percent / 100)            # per hit
per hit   ×= level_modifier(0.5–1.5) × defense_mult(1 − def/(def+500))
if crit (rand% < CRITCHANCE):  × ( rand(1.2,1.5) + CRITDAMAGE/100 )
  • ATTACK_POWER = WEAPONATTACK (melee/bow) or MAGICATTACK (staff). Base 0 — 100% gear-driven; it is the single biggest damage lever. Model assumes ≈ 2×level (anchored to the L100 benchmark ≈197).
  • primary/secondary = base 4 + allocated attributes (5/level pool) + mastery (discipline bonus × mastery level). No per-level auto scaling — it is all manual now.
  • CRITDAMAGE base 0 with no gear source → a crit is only ×1.2–1.5. Crit rate is a near-dead stat.
  • Tables below are raw, same-level, zero-defense single-target to isolate ability balance (defense & level-gap are flat multipliers that hit every ability equally).

Attribute allocation archetypes

The 5-points/level pool (= 5×(level−1); 495 at L100) is spent manually. Three builds modelled:

  • default — discipline ratio (what an un-respecced character auto-gets; e.g. Sword 3 STR : 2 DEX). Reproduces the old baseline.
  • pure — every point into the primary stat. The damage-max build.
  • tank — half primary, half CON (survivability at a damage cost).

Balance findings & recommendations

1 · Crit is a dead stat (CRITDAMAGE base 0, no gear source)

A crit only multiplies ×1.2–1.5. LUCK→crit-rate (Dagger primary, +0.1%/pt) therefore barely moves DPS, and the Dagger's Shadowmeld guaranteed crit is worth far less than it sounds. Fix B6: add CRITDAMAGE rolls to high-tier gear so crit builds (and the guaranteed-crit ambush) actually pay off. Until then Dagger's LUCK-primary identity is mostly a non-lever.

2 · Pure-primary allocation ≈ +40% damage over default

Re-speccing all points into the primary stat is a large, always-correct DPS gain with no opportunity cost but survivability. Either soft-cap primary scaling, give the secondary stat a real damage role, or make CON/utility compelling enough to tempt points away. (ADR 0002 Open #1.)

3 · Finishers read correctly as finishers

Snipe (Bow, 200%+10/lvl, consumes Momentum), Eviscerate (Dagger, 190%+10/lvl, stealth execute) and Sundering Blow (Sword, combo spender) top their kits' single-hit damage, gated behind cooldown / a resource. Good shape — the payoff abilities hit hardest.

4 · DoT abilities under-represented in raw tables

Hemorrhage / Barbed Shot / Envenom / Immolate show only their direct hit here; their bleed/poison/burn DoT adds 20%-of-stat/sec ×stacks on top (and Immolate under Fire stance roughly doubles + splashes). Their effective DPS is higher than the table — keep that in mind before buffing their base %.

5 · Spammable starters are low-CD DPS floors — watch mana

Arcane Bolt (1.2s), Twin Fang (1.0s), Steel Flurry (1.0s) and Split Shot (1.5s) deliver high sustained DPS at trivial mana. They set each kit's floor; the cooldown abilities must clear that floor (per-cast) to be worth a button. Most do; verify Cripple (10s CD, 70% dmg) earns its slot beyond the debuff utility.

6 · Cross-weapon parity holds at the starter level, diverges by build

At default allocation the four kits land within a reasonable band. Staff scales on MAGICATTACK (separate gear pool) and leans on its element stance for effective output; Bow's Momentum ramp (+35% dmg / +30% fire-rate at cap) and Dagger's ambush ×2 are multiplicative on top of these raw numbers, so signature uptime — not base % — is where real divergence will come from in play.

7 · Tune targets, not just numbers

Key starting-value knobs flagged this session: Immolate Fire splash %, Glacial Spike freeze duration, lightning chain hops, Snipe's +12%/stack, Eviscerate's 35% execute threshold, Momentum per-stack ramp. All are isolated constants — cheap to adjust from playtest feel.

- **doc note:** sword finishers spend combo at +100%%/point (x4; Power Strike x7) per COMBO_DAMAGE_PER_POINT's deliberate tuning history - the sword_combo.gd header said +25%%/point and has been corrected. This finisher ceiling is the main driver of the sword-vs-dagger effective gap; rebalance is a design call.
- **element imbalance:** saturated FIRE burn 170 vs LIGHTNING 67 per hit at Staff L10 (x2.5) — plus ICE deals 0 (utility-only by design, but it competes with these for the same stance slot).

Tile role tagger — Country Village (depth-shaded terrain)

The ground is depth-shaded: grass on top → stone underneath → fades to a black core. Pick a role on the left, then click or drag tiles to tag them (a role can hold several variants). Right-click a tile to clear it. Essential: the grass-top edges + corners, the stone body (left / fill / right), and the single black core. Optional: stone underside, inner corners, and decor (switch to the Props sheet). When done, Copy JSON and paste it back to me.
